FAQ

How do I install the TV app?
  1. On your Google TV or Android TV, install the free Downloader app from the Google Play Store.
  2. Let Downloader install apps: Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → turn on Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader, type 6963558 and press Go. When the download finishes, choose Install.
  4. Open NitroVision TV and pick your services. That's it.
Is it free and legal?

Yes. NitroVision is free, with no ads. It never hosts or streams video. It shows what's on the official services you already have, and opens them in their own apps.

Do I need to give it my streaming logins?

No. You never enter a login or password into NitroVision, in the app or the add-on. You sign in inside each service's own app, as you normally would. NitroVision only stores your choice of countries and services.

Does it work on Fire TV or phones?

The NitroVision TV beta is built for Google TV and Android TV. It isn't tested on Fire TV, and it isn't designed for phones or tablets. The Stremio add-on works on any device that runs Stremio, including phones, PCs and Fire TV.

Why does Netflix ask who's watching first?

Netflix usually asks who's watching when another app opens it. Pick your profile and Netflix goes on to the title you chose.

How do I update or uninstall the beta?

The app checks for updates when it opens and offers to install them. You can also go to Settings → Check for updates, or reinstall with Downloader code 6963558. To uninstall, go to your TV's Settings → Apps → NitroVision TV → Uninstall.
