Inside the TV App

Six things your TV does when you let it.

01 · Idle screen

It never just sits there.

A famous painting or a space picture, with the time, weather and today's plans.

When nobody is watching, your TV turns into a calm, useful screen. A famous painting or a space picture fills the display, with the time, the weather, today's plans, bin day (every 2 to 4 weeks) and the family to-do list on top. Scan the QR code with a phone to add a to-do from anywhere in the room.

02 · Free channels

Live TV, right inside.

Free channels that play full screen inside the app.

Free channels play right inside NitroVision, full screen, with no other app to open. The guide shows what is on now and next across news, movies, entertainment, science and music. Pick a provider, mark your favourites and show only those, and see three live channels on Home. Never shown on children's profiles.

03 · Family tile

Make your family a tile.

Your own tile, with your colours and characters.

Type your family name, pick a colour and a style, then add a family of characters from themed packs. It becomes your own tile on Home, so the app feels like it belongs to your house.

04 · Kids Home

A safe Home for the kids.

Their own Home with only U and PG titles.

Children's profiles get their own Home, with only titles rated U or PG and rows made for kids. There is no Live TV on a child's profile, so news and films for adults never turn up.

05 · Pick a film

Pick it. Press Play.

See what it is, then open it in your service's own app.

Open any film or show to see what it is: the story, the rating, the trailer and which of your services has it. Press Play and it opens in that service's own app, whether that is Netflix, Disney+, iPlayer or another, so you never hunt through ten apps.

06 · No app needed

Your phones join in.

Add plans and to-dos from any phone.

Scan the QR code on the TV and your phone opens a simple page. Add events and to-dos for the family, with no download and no sign-in. They show up on the Idle screen for everyone.

Installing the TV App

Not currently in the Google Play Store yet.

The TV App is in beta, so it is not on the Google Play Store yet. You install it with a free app called Downloader, which is. It takes about a minute.

  • Is it safe? Downloader is a well-known app from the Play Store, and the file comes from this site. You can remove NitroVision TV any time: TV Settings, Apps, NitroVision TV, Uninstall.
  • Your TV may ask you to allow it. Turn on Downloader under Settings, Apps, Security and restrictions, Unknown sources. Turn it off again afterwards if you like.
  • On your phone right now? Open the download page on your TV by typing 6963558 into Downloader. The code is the same for everyone.

Questions

FAQ

How do I install the TV app?
  1. On your Google TV or Android TV, install the free Downloader app from the Google Play Store.
  2. Let Downloader install apps: Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → turn on Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader, type 6963558 and press Go. When the download finishes, choose Install.
  4. Open NitroVision TV and pick your services. That's it.
Is it free and legal?

Yes. NitroVision is free and shows no ads of its own. (Your streaming services may still show their own ads, depending on your plan: NitroVision does not remove them.) It never hosts or streams video. It shows what's on the official services you already have, and opens them in their own apps.

Do I need to give it my streaming logins?

No. You never enter a login or password into NitroVision, in the app or the add-on. You sign in inside each service's own app, as you normally would. NitroVision only stores your choice of countries and services.

Does it work on Fire TV or phones?

The NitroVision TV beta is built for Google TV and Android TV. It isn't tested on Fire TV, and it isn't designed for phones or tablets. The Stremio add-on works on any device that runs Stremio, including phones, PCs and Fire TV.

Why does Netflix ask who's watching first?

Netflix usually asks who's watching when another app opens it. Pick your profile and Netflix goes on to the title you chose.

How do I update or uninstall the beta?

The app checks for updates when it opens and offers to install them. You can also go to Settings → Check for updates, or reinstall with Downloader code 6963558. To uninstall, go to your TV's Settings → Apps → NitroVision TV → Uninstall.
