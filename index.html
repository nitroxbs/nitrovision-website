NitroVision automatically checks whether the movie or TV show selected through Stremio is available through supported legal streaming providers across the countries you choose. Pick the services you already subscribe to, and NitroVision opens that provider's app or website directly — plus adds the catalogue rows you choose, from trending and new releases to a row for each of your services, and live public-broadcaster TV channels to your Stremio home screen.

Checks legal availability

Automatically looks up the movie or series across supported streaming services using real-time availability data, so the link you get points straight to the title, not just the provider's homepage.

Multiple countries, your way

Select as many countries as you have access to — the UK, US, Canada, Australia and more — and tick exactly which services you subscribe to in each one individually.

Opens the provider directly

When you select an available option, NitroVision launches the provider's app or website so you can continue watching there — including launching the right app directly on Android TV and Google TV.

Catalogs & Live TV

🎬 A row for every service

New in v1.5.0: every service you tick gets its own Movies and TV Shows rows for your region — Netflix Movies, BBC iPlayer TV Shows, Channel 4 Movies, Hulu TV Shows and so on — so you can browse each one like its own app.

📺 Pick your catalogues

Trending, Popular, New Movies, Recent Releases, Top Rated All-Time, Family & Kids, Documentaries, True Crime, Anime and an hourly Super Mix — all filtered to what's on your services. Keep the Recommended set, or choose your own. New in v1.6.0: NitroVision results on Stremio's search page too.

📡 NitroVision Live TV

Real, individual live channels — BBC One, BBC Two, ITV1, Channel 4, STV and more in the UK, plus free US news and FAST channels — each one shown with its own channel logo and linking straight to the broadcaster's own live player. Free public-broadcaster TV, no subscription needed.

NitroVision TV

NitroVision TV for Google TVBeta 1.1.0

A standalone NitroVision app for your TV: browse your NitroVision rows and Live TV with the remote, pick a title, and press Play to jump straight into the service's own app to watch.

New in Beta 1.1.0: a first-run setup for your countries, services and live channels; profiles for everyone in the house, each with their own Watchlist, rows and services (or the household's master list); a new home screen with your services up top; search; reorder or hide rows and services any time; and Check for updates in Settings.

This is a beta. It's an early test build for Google TV and Android TV devices only, and it isn't tested on phones, tablets or Fire TV. Expect bugs, rough edges and missing features. It may be unstable, and it may change or stop working without notice. Install it at your own risk.

Already on Beta 1.0.0? Install 1.1.0 over it the same way. You'll go through the new setup the first time it opens. From 1.1.0 on, Settings → Check for updates installs new betas for you.
