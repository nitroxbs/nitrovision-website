Legal streaming options,
right inside Stremio.

NitroVision automatically checks whether the movie or TV show selected through Stremio is available through supported legal streaming providers across the countries you choose. Pick the services you already subscribe to, and NitroVision opens that provider's app or website directly — plus adds personalized trending catalogs and live public-broadcaster TV channels to your Stremio home screen.

Configure & Install

NitroVision needs to know your country and providers before it can generate your personal install link — click Configure & Install to set that up, it only takes a minute.

Checks legal availability

Automatically looks up the movie or series across supported streaming services using real-time availability data, so the link you get points straight to the title, not just the provider's homepage.

Multiple countries, your way

Select as many countries as you have access to — the UK, US, Canada, Australia and more — and tick exactly which services you subscribe to in each one individually.

Opens the provider directly

When you select an available option, NitroVision launches the provider's app or website so you can continue watching there — including launching the right app directly on Android TV and Google TV.

Catalogs & Live TV

🎬 NitroVision Movies

Trending movies pulled live from across every provider you selected in the configurator — a personalized "what's popular" row built entirely from services you actually pay for.

📺 NitroVision TV Shows

The same idea, for TV — trending shows across Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and whatever else you've ticked, refreshed automatically.

📡 NitroVision TV

A separate row just for public broadcaster catch-up — BBC iPlayer, ITVX, Channel 4, 5, S4C Clic and similar — so reality shows and live broadcast content don't get buried under the big commercial streamers.

📡 NitroVision Live TV

New: real, individual live channels — BBC One, BBC Two, ITV1, Channel 4, STV and more in the UK, plus free US news and FAST channels — each one shown with its own channel logo and linking straight to the broadcaster's own live player. Free public-broadcaster TV, no subscription needed.
