FAQ

Is it free and legal?

Yes. NitroVision is free, with no ads. It never hosts or streams video. It shows what's on the official services you already have, and opens them in their own apps.

Do I need to give it my streaming logins?

No. You never enter a login or password into NitroVision. You sign in inside each service's own app, as you normally would. NitroVision only stores your choice of countries and services.

Does it work on Fire TV or phones?

The NitroVision TV beta is built for Google TV and Android TV. It isn't tested on Fire TV, and it isn't designed for phones or tablets. The Stremio add-on works on any device that runs Stremio, including phones, PCs and Fire TV.

Why does Netflix ask who's watching first?

Netflix always asks which profile is watching when another app opens it. Pick your profile and Netflix goes on to the title you chose.

How do I update or uninstall the beta?

From Beta 1.3.0, the app checks for updates when it opens and offers to install them. You can also go to Settings → Check for updates (from Beta 1.1.0 on). You can also reinstall with Downloader code 6963558. To uninstall, go to your TV's Settings → Apps → NitroVision TV → Uninstall.
